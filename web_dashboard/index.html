Teacher Viva Key Talking Points:

  • Why Voltage Divider on GPIO 34? The MQ-135 is powered by 5V, so its analog output can reach above 3.3V. ESP32 ADC pins can only safely accept maximum 3.3V (0-3.3V input range). Feeding 5V directly damages the ESP32 silicon!
  • Why GPIO 12 for DHT11? GPIO 12 is a bootstrap pin (MTDI). If held high at reset, flash voltage might switch to 1.8V instead of 3.3V. Our firmware and wiring ensure safe operation after power-on.
  • Why GPIO 22 & 23 for OLED? Default ESP32 I2C pins are 21 and 22, but since GPIO 21 is assigned to the Buzzer, we route SCL to GPIO 23 and SDA to GPIO 22 using Wire.begin(22, 23).
